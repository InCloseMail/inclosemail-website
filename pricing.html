Questions before you roll it out

Do we have to switch Microsoft 365 plans or migrate our email?

No. InCloseMail signs in to your existing Microsoft 365 work accounts. Your mail, calendars and contacts stay where they are.

How many licenses do we need if several people use the same shared mailbox?

One for each person who uses InCloseMail. The shared mailboxes they already have access to are included.

Can I use one license on my desktop and laptop?

Yes. A license covers one person on up to three Windows PCs.

When does the trial start, and what happens when it ends?

During the beta, work accounts are free and there's no trial to start. After beta, the 30-day trial starts when you connect your first work account. When it ends without a license, work accounts become read-only: you can still read, search, print and export the mail already on your PC, but nothing syncs, sends or changes until you buy a license. Personal accounts stay free either way.
