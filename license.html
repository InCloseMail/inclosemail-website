5. Data collection and privacy

What the Software accesses: to work, the Software signs in to your Microsoft account and, with the permissions you or your organization grant, reads and (when you allow changes) changes your email, calendar, contacts and mailbox settings through Microsoft's services. For a work or school account, it can also read and change shared mailboxes and calendars that you have been given access to, and send mail from shared mailboxes you are allowed to send from. It accesses these only to provide the Software's features.

Where your data goes: the Software connects directly from your PC to Microsoft's sign-in and Microsoft Graph services. It does not send your email, calendar, contacts, account details or sign-in information to us or to any other third party. This beta contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash-reporting code. Apart from Microsoft's services, the only connection it makes on its own is the update check described below.

Update checks: unless you turn it off in File > Options > General, once a day at launch the Software asks GitHub (api.github.com) whether a newer version is available, and it downloads an update from GitHub (github.com) only when you choose to install it. GitHub receives your IP address and the Software's version number, and handles them under its own privacy statement. Help > Check for Updates asks only when you choose it.

What is stored on your PC: the Software keeps the following in your Windows user profile. Encrypted with Windows data protection for your Windows user: your sign-in information and the local copy (cache) of your mailbox. Not encrypted: your settings (which include email addresses, such as your Safe Senders list), your signatures, a short sign-in log (which records whether sign-in worked, without addresses, names or passwords), the message viewer's working files (which can include pictures you chose to download), and temporary copies of attachments you open (deleted after a day, or when you sign out). If you sign in with an account added to Windows, Windows also keeps that account and its sign-in tokens in its own protected store.

Removing it: signing out removes your sign-in information, the local copy of your mailbox and the temporary attachment copies; your settings, signatures and sign-in log stay. Uninstalling the Software removes all of the above for the Windows user who uninstalls it, unless that user clears the option to remove their data; it does not remove other Windows users' data on the same PC. Accounts added to Windows itself stay in Windows.

Other connections: pictures and other remote content in messages are blocked unless you choose to download them; if you do, the senders' servers can see that the message was opened. Links in messages open in your web browser only when you click them. New-mail alerts are shown through Windows notifications, which Windows manages. Messages are displayed with Microsoft Edge WebView2, a Windows component that may itself contact Microsoft, for example for security checks and updates, under Microsoft's terms.

Information you send us: if you choose to contact us or send feedback, we receive what you send (such as your email address, your message and any logs or screenshots you attach) and use it only to support the beta and improve the Software. We do not sell it.

Changes: if a later version collects any additional data (for example, to manage licenses), we will describe it before that version collects it.
