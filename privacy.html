InCloseMail Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

InCloseMail is a desktop email client for Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com on Windows, published by EmuKey LLC, a Wisconsin limited liability company (“we”). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your mail stays between your PC and Microsoft, and we never receive it.

What the app accesses

InCloseMail signs in with your Microsoft account. With the permissions you or your organization grant, it reads and, when you allow changes, changes your email, calendar, contacts and mailbox settings. For a work or school account, it can also read and change shared mailboxes and calendars you have been given access to, and send from shared mailboxes you are allowed to send from. It accesses these only to provide the app’s features.

Where your data goes

The app connects directly from your PC to Microsoft’s sign-in service and to Microsoft Graph. It does not send your email, calendar, contacts, account details or sign-in information to EmuKey LLC or to any other third party. The current version has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting, and it doesn’t contact our servers.

Pictures and other remote content in messages are blocked unless you choose to download them for a message. If you do, the sender’s servers can see that the message was opened. Links in messages open in your web browser only when you click them. Messages are displayed with Microsoft Edge WebView2, a Windows component that may itself contact Microsoft, for example for security checks and updates, under Microsoft’s terms.

What’s stored on your PC

  • Encrypted with Windows data protection for your Windows user: your sign-in tokens and the local copy (cache) of your mailbox.
  • Not encrypted: your settings (which include email addresses, such as your Safe Senders list), your signatures, a short sign-in log that records whether sign-in worked (without addresses, names or passwords), the message viewer’s working files, and temporary copies of attachments you open (deleted after a day, or when you sign out).

Signing out removes your sign-in tokens, the mailbox copy and the temporary attachment copies. Uninstalling the app removes the rest of this data for the Windows user who uninstalls it, unless they choose to keep it.

If you contact us

If you email support@inclosemail.com or send feedback, we receive what you send, such as your email address, your message and any logs or screenshots you attach. We use it only to support you and improve the app. We don’t sell it.

How to remove the app’s access

  1. In InCloseMail, choose File → Sign Out.
  2. Remove the app’s permissions from your account:
    • Work or school account: myapps.microsoft.com
    • Personal Microsoft account: account.live.com/consent/Manage

This website

inclosemail.com uses no analytics, no tracking and no cookies. If you use the Dark mode button, your choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) and never sent anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are served, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

If a later version of InCloseMail collects any additional data, for example to check for updates, we will describe it before that version does so. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
