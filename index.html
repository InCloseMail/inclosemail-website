InCloseMail

If you’ve used classic Outlook, you already know how to use InCloseMail.

A Windows desktop email client for Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com that works the way classic Outlook does: the ribbon, the menus, the right-click options and the keyboard shortcuts you already use, for mail, calendar, contacts and tasks.

Download the free beta All features Help What’s new

Try it with no email account: choose Try demo mode when it starts and look around a full sample mailbox, calendar and contacts.

Windows 10 (version 1809) or Windows 11, 64-bit. Free for personal use. Signed installer. Updates install from inside the app.

Classic Outlook, feature for feature

InCloseMail was checked against classic Outlook, screen by screen: if you reach for something out of habit, it’s where you expect it.

  • The layout you knowThe ribbon and its tabs, the folder list with Favorites, the message list, the reading pane, the To-Do Bar, Tell me, and Outlook’s keyboard shortcuts.
  • Mail the way you workConversations, categories, follow-up flags and reminders, Ignore, Clean Up, search folders, Advanced Find, and the People Pane.
  • CalendarDay, week and month views, meetings with the Scheduling Assistant and Room Finder, shared calendars, calendar groups and internet calendars.
  • Contacts and tasksYour contacts with contact groups and colleagues’ shared contacts, and tasks with due dates, reminders and recurrence.
  • Rules and Quick StepsRules that run on the server, Quick Steps with Outlook’s full list of actions, Always Move Messages in This Conversation, and rules you can export and import.
  • Writing messagesSignatures, Quick Parts, voting buttons, Delay Delivery, Format Painter and styles, spell check, and Save Sent Item To.
  • Several accounts, shared mailboxes and GroupsYour work and personal accounts side by side, shared mailboxes and calendars with Send As, Microsoft 365 Groups, and search across all of your mailboxes.
  • Focused InboxFocused and Other, as in Outlook for Microsoft 365, with Always Move to Other for senders you don’t need to see first.
  • Offline and Send/ReceiveA local copy of your mail that stays readable and searchable offline, Work Offline, and Send/Receive with Show Progress.
  • Bring your .pst filesOpen an Outlook Data File of any size, import it into your mailbox, or export your mail to one, all without Outlook.
  • “Why is this suspicious?”A plain-language explanation for messages that look like phishing.
  • A second look before sendingA check for forgotten attachments, outside recipients and replies to many people.
  • Read-only when you want itMake any account read-only, so nothing in it can change, or allow only the kinds of change you choose.
  • Signed and encrypted mailRead S/MIME messages and sign or encrypt your own, with your Windows certificates.
  • Light, dark and moreLight and Dark Mode, High Contrast, and holiday themes that change with the season.
  • Ready for ITA signed Windows Installer package and Group Policy and Intune templates for managed deployment.

About the beta

InCloseMail will always be free for personal use. With an Outlook.com, Hotmail, Live or other personal Microsoft account, you’ll never pay for it. Only Microsoft 365 work and business accounts will need a license once the beta ends. During the beta, it’s free for every account.

Each new build is offered inside the app (Help → Check for Updates). Beta builds work until December 31, 2026; after that, a beta build keeps the mail already on your PC readable but stops sending and receiving until you install a newer version.

Found a problem, or missing something you used in Outlook? Use Help → Suggest a Feature or Contact Support in the app.
