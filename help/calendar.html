Views

  • The Calendar Home tab has Day, Work Week, Week, Month and Schedule View (Ctrl+Alt+1 to Ctrl+Alt+5), plus Today and Next 7 Days.
  • Change View lists your items, and Time Scale sets how much time each row covers.
  • Click a date in the small month calendar in the folder pane to go there. Ctrl+G opens Go To Date.
  • File → Options → Calendar sets your work hours, work week, first day of the week, a second time zone, week numbers and the default reminder. Add Holidays is there too.

Appointments and meetings

  • New appointment: Ctrl+Shift+A. New meeting: Ctrl+Shift+Q. Ctrl+N makes a new appointment while you’re in Calendar.
  • Click an item to see its details. Double-click it to open the form. If the account is read-only, so is the form.
  • Drag an item to move it. Hold Ctrl while dragging in the Day and Week views to copy it.
  • Answer invitations with Accept, Tentative or Decline. Forward sends a meeting to more people.
  • The meeting form has the Scheduling Assistant, and Rooms beside Location opens Room Finder.
  • Appointments and meetings can have recurrence and attached files.
  • Reminders appear in the Reminders window, where you can snooze or dismiss them. They need a connection.

Other calendars

  • My Calendars: tick one to show it over your main calendar in its own color. New Calendar (Home) adds a blank one.
  • Open Shared Calendar (Home) shows a colleague’s calendar or a shared mailbox’s by email address. These open read-only here.
  • Share Calendar (Home) shows who can see your calendar and lets you add, change or remove people.
  • Calendar From Internet (Home) adds a published .ics link, such as public holidays or a team’s schedule. It’s read-only, and it stays on this PC, so Outlook on other PCs won’t show it.
  • Calendar Groups (Home) shows several calendars together.

Daily Task List

Under the Day, Work Week and Week views, it shows the tasks and flagged messages due each day. Overdue ones show on today. Tick one to complete it, or double-click it to open it. View → Daily Task List → Off hides it.

Printing

File → Print or Ctrl+P, with a preview of the pages. Settings offers Outlook’s styles: Daily, Weekly Agenda, Weekly Calendar, Monthly, Tri-fold and Calendar Details.
