November 7, 2026

InCloseMail Beta, first public release version 26.11.703

A Windows email client for Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com that works the way Outlook 2021 does: the ribbon, the menus, the right-click options and the keyboard shortcuts you already know, for mail, calendar, contacts and tasks.

  • Mail: conversations, Focused Inbox, categories, follow-up flags, rules, Quick Steps, search folders, Advanced Find and Clean Up.
  • Calendar: day, week, month and schedule views, meetings with the Scheduling Assistant, Room Finder and Teams, shared and internet calendars.
  • People and tasks: contacts, contact groups and your Microsoft To Do tasks.
  • Several accounts at once, shared mailboxes with Send As, and Microsoft 365 Groups.
  • Offline: your mail stays on your PC, readable and searchable without a connection.
  • Your .pst files: open, import and export Outlook Data Files without Outlook.
  • Safer mail: “Why is this suspicious?” for phishing, a check before sending, S/MIME signing and encryption, and read-only mode until you allow changes.
  • Light, dark, high contrast and holiday themes.
