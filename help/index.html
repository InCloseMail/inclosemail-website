If you’ve used classic Outlook, most of InCloseMail is already familiar: the ribbon, the menus, the right-click options and the keyboard shortcuts are where you expect them. These pages cover getting started and the places where InCloseMail works differently.

Get started

  1. Install it. Download InCloseMail.msi and run it. You need Windows 10 (version 1809) or Windows 11, 64-bit. If message text doesn’t show after installing, see Troubleshooting.
  2. Sign in. Use a Microsoft 365 work or school account, or an Outlook.com account. The first time, Microsoft asks you to allow the app’s permissions, all of them at once, so you aren’t asked again later. Your organization may need an administrator to approve them (For administrators).
  3. Coming from classic Outlook? See Moving from classic Outlook to bring your signatures across and open your old .pst files.
  4. Start using it. Your mail, calendar and contacts are ready to use, as in Outlook. Want to look around first without changing anything? Make the account read-only; see Read-only mode.

Just want to look? Choose Try demo mode on the sign-in screen. It opens a sample mailbox, calendar and contacts that you can change freely, with no account.

Find your way around

  • Switch areas with the Mail, Calendar, People and Tasks buttons at the bottom of the folder pane, or Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+4.
  • Find any command by typing a few words in the Tell me what you want to do box to the right of the ribbon tabs (Alt+Q).
  • Open help for the area you’re in by pressing F1.

Different from Outlook

  • Read-only when you want it. Any account can be made read-only, or allowed only some kinds of change. See Read-only mode.
  • Settings stay on this PC. Options, Quick Steps, signatures, Quick Parts, templates and Safe Senders aren’t stored in your mailbox, so Outlook and your other PCs keep their own. Rules, categories and Automatic Replies are in your mailbox and are shared.
  • Attachments are never stored on this PC ahead of time, and the local copy keeps 30 days of mail unless you change it. See Offline and the local copy.
  • Contact groups are InCloseMail’s own. Outlook’s contact groups don’t appear, as Microsoft 365 doesn’t share them with other apps.
  • A few things aren’t available because Microsoft doesn’t offer them to other apps, including message recall and room lists.
