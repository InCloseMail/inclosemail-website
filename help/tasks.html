Tasks shows your Microsoft To Do lists, the same tasks you see in Outlook on the web and new Outlook. Press Ctrl+4 or click Tasks at the bottom of the folder pane.

The first time

Sign-in asks for the Tasks permission with the rest. If it wasn’t granted then, the first click opens a Microsoft sign-in window for it. If you cancel, Tasks explains why and offers Allow access. Your organization may need an administrator to approve it.

Using tasks

  • Type in Click here to add a new Task and press Enter. New Task (Ctrl+N, or Ctrl+Shift+K from anywhere) opens the full form.
  • File → Options → Tasks has Set reminders on tasks with due dates: a task you give a due date on the form then gets a reminder that day, at the Default reminder time (8:00 AM unless you change it).
  • The form has the start and due dates, status, priority, reminder, categories, notes and Recurrence. Completing a recurring task brings the next one.
  • Follow Up on the form sets the start and due dates in one click: Today, Tomorrow, This Week, Next Week or No Date. Custom picks dates and a reminder, Add Reminder turns the reminder on, and Mark Complete completes the task.
  • Attach File on the form (pick from Recent Items or Browse This PC), or files dropped on it, attach files to the task. They’re saved with the task in Microsoft To Do and open with a click. Change an opened file and save it in its app, and Save & Close keeps the change.
  • Attach Item on the form attaches messages (Outlook Item) and contacts (Business Card) from your mailbox to the task. Calendar puts your calendar for the days you choose into the notes.
  • Forward on the form opens a new message with the task’s details and its files. Send Status Report does the same without the files, titled “Task Status Report”.
  • Zoom on the form makes the notes’ text larger or smaller, from 50% to 200%.
  • The check box on a row, Mark Complete or Insert completes a task or reopens it.
  • My Tasks in the folder pane lists your task lists, and those of any account added beside yours. If an added account hasn’t allowed Tasks yet, click Allow access there.
  • Change View has Simple List, Detailed, To-Do List, Prioritized, Active, Completed, Today, Next 7 Days and Overdue.
  • To-Do List shows your open tasks and flagged messages together, by due date.

To-Do Bar

View → To-Do Bar keeps the calendar, your favorite people and your flagged mail beside your messages in Mail: check Calendar, People or Tasks, in any mix. People has Search People and your favorites, as the People peek; click a person for their contact card.
