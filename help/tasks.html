Tasks shows your Microsoft To Do lists, the same tasks you see in Outlook on the web and new Outlook. Press Ctrl+4 or click Tasks at the bottom of the folder pane.

The first time

Tasks needs a permission that sign-in doesn’t ask for, so the first click opens a Microsoft sign-in window for it. If you cancel, Tasks explains why and offers Allow access. Your organization may need an administrator to approve it.

Using tasks

  • Type in Click here to add a new Task and press Enter. New Task (Ctrl+N, or Ctrl+Shift+K from anywhere) opens the full form.
  • The form has the start and due dates, status, priority, reminder, categories, notes and Recurrence.... Completing a recurring task brings the next one.
  • The check box on a row, Mark Complete or Insert completes a task or reopens it.
  • My Tasks in the folder pane lists your task lists.
  • Change View has Simple List, Detailed, To-Do List, Prioritized, Active, Completed, Today, Next 7 Days and Overdue.
  • To-Do List shows your open tasks and flagged messages together, by due date.

To-Do Bar

View → To-Do Bar keeps the calendar and your flagged mail beside your messages in Mail.
