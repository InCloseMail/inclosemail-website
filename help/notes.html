Notes are InCloseMail’s sticky notes, as in classic Outlook: short notes for things you’d jot on a slip of paper. Press Ctrl+5 or click Notes at the bottom of the folder pane.

Writing a note

  • New Note (Ctrl+N in Notes, or Ctrl+Shift+N from anywhere, or New Items → Note) opens a small note window. Just type: the first line is the note’s title.
  • A note saves itself a moment after you stop typing, and again when you close it (Esc or ×). An empty new note isn’t kept.
  • Drag a note by its top strip and size it from its corner. Notes stay open beside InCloseMail while you work.
  • The menu at the top left of a note has Forward, Color (blue, green, pink, yellow or white), Categorize, Delete and Close.

Finding notes

  • Icon shows each note as a little note of its color. Notes List shows a row for each with its text and when it was last changed. Last 7 Days shows only the notes changed this past week.
  • The box over the notes searches their text and categories.
  • Double-click a note (or select it and press Enter) to open it. Right-click it for Forward, Color, Categorize and Delete. Delete also works on its own key.
  • Forward opens a new message with the note’s text, its first line as the subject.

Where notes are kept

Your notes are kept in your own mailbox, in a hidden folder named InCloseMail Notes, so InCloseMail shows the same notes on every PC you use it on. Nothing goes to a server of ours. Refresh loads them again if you changed them on another PC.

Outlook, Outlook on the web and Windows Sticky Notes don’t show these notes, and InCloseMail can’t show theirs: Microsoft’s connection for apps (Microsoft Graph) doesn’t reach Outlook’s own Notes folder. A deleted note can be brought back for a while with Recover Deleted Items. In demo mode, the sample notes are kept on your PC.
