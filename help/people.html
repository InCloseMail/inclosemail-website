Contacts

  • Press Ctrl+3 or click People at the bottom of the folder pane.
  • Change View (Home) switches between People, Business Card, Phone and List.
  • The search box above the list matches names, companies, addresses and phone numbers. The A–Z index beside the list jumps to a letter.
  • New Contact is Ctrl+Shift+C. Double-click a contact to edit it. Delete sends it to Deleted Items, after asking.
  • Contact folders appear under My Contacts. New Folder (Home) adds one.
  • File → Options → People sets the sort order and how names are filed.

Contact groups

New Contact Group (Ctrl+Shift+L) gives a name to several people. Type that name in To, Cc or Bcc to send to everyone in it. These groups are kept on this PC. Outlook’s own contact groups don’t appear, as Microsoft 365 doesn’t share them with other apps.

Address Book

Ctrl+Shift+B opens the Address Book. It shows your contacts or, for a work or school account, the Global Address List. If the permission to read names and addresses wasn’t granted when you signed in, Allow asks for it.

More

  • Search People (Home) finds someone by name or address from Mail too.
  • Mail Merge (People → Home) sends one message per contact, with merge fields.
  • Open Shared Contacts shows contacts someone has shared with you.
  • Follow Up flags a contact, as in Outlook.
