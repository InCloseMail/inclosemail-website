InCloseMail uses Outlook’s keys. These are the ones people use most.

Everywhere

KeysWhat they do
F1Help for the area you’re in
Alt+QTell me what you want to do
Ctrl+1, 2, 3, 4Mail, Calendar, People, Tasks
Ctrl+NA new item of the kind you’re looking at
Ctrl+OOpen the selected item: a message, appointment, contact or task
Alt+Left or Alt+B, Alt+RightBack to the view you were in before, and forward again
Ctrl+Shift+MNew message
Ctrl+Shift+A, Ctrl+Shift+QNew appointment, new meeting
Ctrl+Shift+C, Ctrl+Shift+KNew contact, new task
Ctrl+E or F3Search
Ctrl+Shift+FAdvanced Find
Ctrl+Alt+K, Ctrl+Alt+Z, Ctrl+Alt+ASearch the current folder, its subfolders, or the whole mailbox
Ctrl+YGo to a folder
F9, Shift+F9Send/Receive all folders, the current folder
Ctrl+PPrint (File → Print, with a preview)
Ctrl+Alt+VPaste Special, in a message
Ctrl+Shift+BAddress Book
F11Search People
Ctrl+Alt+SSend/Receive settings
F6, Shift+F6Move between the panes (Mail)
Ctrl+0Reset the window’s zoom

Ribbon key tips

Press Alt or F10 and each tab and command shows its letters, as in Outlook. For example, Alt, H, R, P replies and Alt, H, D deletes.
